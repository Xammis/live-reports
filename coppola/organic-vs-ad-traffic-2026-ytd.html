03 · Method

Simple definitions, clear boundaries

The report uses GA4 session-level acquisition groups and the latest complete reporting day.

Metric

Sessions

Traffic means GA4 sessions, not users, clicks, impressions, or conversions.

Organic

Organic Search

The organic side contains GA4's Organic Search default channel group only.

Advertising

All paid groups present

Paid Search, Display, Cross-network, and Paid Other are combined as advertising.

Window

Year to date

January 1 through August 5, 2026. August is a five-day partial period.

Data handling: headline totals come from the direct full-range GA4 query. Monthly rows are period-level views and should not be summed to reconstruct the headline because GA4 session metrics can be non-additive across time dimensions. Exact session counts were delivered privately in Basecamp and intentionally excluded from this public report.

04 · Actions

Protect the organic engine. Measure paid impact.

The channel mix supports a balanced plan: defend the traffic source that supplies scale while tightening the evidence behind advertising decisions.

Protect

Investigate organic softness

Review landing pages, rankings, indexation, and seasonality to understand why organic's monthly share narrowed after February.

Prove

Connect ads to outcomes

Pair engagement with qualified conversions, cost per result, campaign, search term, and landing-page performance.

Repeat

Run a monthly acquisition pulse

Track channel share, engagement, conversion quality, and campaign concentration on the same calendar basis.
