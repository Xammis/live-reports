Highest-leverage next move: confirm the removal date and intended redirect destination, then audit remaining Tavern references. Do not restore a retired page just to recover irrelevant visibility. If no relevant replacement exists, review whether an explicit retirement response is more accurate than an unrelated homepage redirect.

09. Next steps and QA

Prioritize hotel demand, conversion paths and a clean benchmark.

1. Recover generic lodging visibility

Start with “Ouray hotels” and related homepage/room-page terms. Check competing results, intent, titles and internal links.

2. Validate completed-booking measurement

Confirm cross-domain reservation attribution, purchase-event deduplication and revenue before reporting bookings or ROI.

3. Preserve the retired-page benchmark

Keep unadjusted property totals plus a labeled ex-Tavern page-table view. Confirm the exact removal date and review redirect relevance.

4. Protect active-page wins

Retain strong coffee, spa, rates and guestroom coverage. Review wedding click efficiency and mobile commercial SERPs.

5. Review seasonal demand without guessing

Use same-month year-over-year and booking data before calling the summer-to-fall trend a purely seasonal effect.
