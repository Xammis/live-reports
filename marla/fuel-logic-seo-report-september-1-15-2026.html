Google lost visibility, not click appeal.

GSC clicks fell 24.3%. Impressions fell 26.1%. CTR improved 2.4%. Average position improved by 1.8. The decline came from fewer search appearances.

GA4 traffic grew, but engagement weakened.

Organic sessions rose 26.8%. Key events fell 8.1%. Engagement rate fell 5.8 points. Average engagement time fell from 40.3 to 23.3 seconds.

Phone outcomes tell the stronger quality story.

Fuel Logic Org+GMB first-time callers rose from 264 to 304. The daily rate rose 22.8%. PPC first-time callers fell by 22, but the daily rate was nearly flat at minus 2.8%.

07. Actions and QA

Recover informational visibility while protecting organic caller growth.

Fix the diesel-grades click gap.

Review query intent, SERP features, and snippet fit. Position improved, but CTR and impressions fell.

Investigate Midlothian CTR.

Position improved from 4.8 to 3.7, but clicks fell from 19 to 8. Check local SERP layout and query mix.

Scale Fuel Logic Org+GMB FTC growth.

Map the 304 first-time callers to pages, profiles, and markets. Use qualified call outcomes to prioritize work.

Audit GA4 traffic quality.

Sessions grew, but engagement and session key-event rate fell. Isolate the high-volume, low-action sources.

Resolve the Fuel Monkey FTC collapse.

FTC fell from 39 to 2. Confirm tracking, routing, and actual demand before treating it as an SEO loss.
