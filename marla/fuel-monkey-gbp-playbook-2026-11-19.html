What changed: fewer free calls from the local pack

2026 local SEO conference research (other businesses, not Fuel Monkey data)

A Smallbiz.Edge study found that over 80% of businesses in the 3-pack no longer showed a call button. One sample had a call button for 510 of 2,580 businesses, or 19.8%.

Ads appeared inside and above the pack, including 3-pack ads and Local Services Ads. One local business received no calls for 1.5 months after Google stopped showing a local pack for its top keywords.

These observations need confirmation against Fuel Monkey’s own numbers before wider use.

What changed: AI selects fewer local businesses

2026 local SEO conference research (other businesses, not Fuel Monkey data)

AI local packs began in early 2026 on US mobile and replaced the traditional pack on about 13% of queries. They later appeared on desktop and in Canada in July 2026; some show 6 businesses.

In 88% of 322 markets checked, AI local packs showed fewer unique businesses than traditional packs. Across 14,000+ Gemini and ChatGPT search citations, 60% pointed to the business’s own website.

One multi-location business reported a lead split of 61% GBP, 37% Google organic and 2% AI chat tools. These are observations from other businesses.

2026 local SEO conference research (other businesses, not Fuel Monkey data)

Reference

Plain-language glossary.

GBP
Google Business Profile, the business listing used in Google Search and Maps.
SAB
Service-area business: a delivery operation that serves customers away from its operating address and hides that address.
Local pack
The map-based group of local businesses shown in Google search results.
AI local pack
An AI-selected group of local businesses shown in a search answer.
UTM
Tracking labels added to a website link so visits and forms can be attributed to their source.
CallRail tracking number
A phone number used to measure where calls came from while keeping the business’s main line available.
Location assets
Google Ads details linked to a profile that can show its address, rating and map pin.
Geo-grid
A fixed set of map points used to compare search visibility across the same area.
PPC
Pay-per-click advertising; the PPC owner manages the ad setup and tests.
LLM / AI information page
The llm-info website page that states business facts for AI tools.
Schema
Structured business information on the website that search and AI tools can read.
