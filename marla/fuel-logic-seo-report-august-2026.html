Executive read

The recovery is clearest in lead outcomes.

Calls and qualified actions rose.

Total calls increased 13.2%. Combined organic calls increased 7.8%. Total KickAss leads reached a period-high 384. Organic KickAss leads reached 237 for a third straight monthly gain.

GA4 organic sessions fell 3.0%, but session-scoped key events rose 3.2%. Average engagement time improved 15.9%.

The channel story improved below the surface.

Overall Google visibility softened. U.S. CTR improved. Local page clicks grew. Bing and DuckDuckGo gained sessions. ChatGPT traffic and key events rose sharply.

Dallas is the local breakout.

Dallas GBP impressions rose 24.1%. Maps impressions rose 47.8%. Phone taps tripled. Total profile conversions rose 21.9%.

The headline decline overstates the commercial impact.

Overall GSC clicks fell 11.7%. U.S. clicks fell only 2.9%. U.S. CTR rose 2.0%. Local page clicks rose 2.6%. Generic “fuel” impressions fell by 8,004 and produced zero clicks in both months.

The smaller audience took more actions.

Key events rose to 548. Session key event rate improved 6.4%. Average engagement time reached 38.1 seconds.

Next move

Protect U.S. commercial and local gains. Put the largest informational declines on a citation and snippet optimization track.

The Dallas reset is landing.

Total impressions rose 24.1%. Maps impressions rose 47.8%. Website clicks rose 33.3%. Phone taps tripled. Total conversions rose 21.9%.

Houston still leads in scale and rating.

Houston held the largest profile conversion volume at 128 and the strongest rating at 4.8 from 57 reviews. Phone taps fell 66.7%. Total conversions fell 26.0%.

Keep the signals separate.

GBP conversions are profile taps counted by Google. CallRail reports calls to tracking numbers. They measure different actions. They should not be forced to match.

Next move

Document the Dallas changes and scale the winning profile elements. Diagnose Houston’s phone and direction declines without changing what still drives website clicks.
