Methodology

All 226 paused campaigns pulled via the Google Ads API, joined against all-time totals and daily metrics for Sep 14, 2025 through Sep 14, 2026. Group 1: last activity within 30 days plus $500+ spend in the last 90 days. Group 2: idle 1-6 months with $1,000+ in the last 12 months. Group 3: everything older or weaker with $500+ lifetime. The 119 paused campaigns under $500 lifetime are counted, not listed. *Conversion columns are inflated by the sitewide page-load tag bug (fix pending with Yong) and, for display, by view-through counting. Use them as relative signal; CallRail KA calls remain the lead truth. Campaign creation dates are not exposed by this API version.
