Read this first: the three patterns that explain almost everything

  • Specialty service lines do not serve. Reefer, Wet Hosing, Generator, and Dyed Diesel campaigns account for 31 of the 44 dormant campaigns (Reefer 10, Wet Hosing 8, Generator 10, Dyed Diesel 3). Search volume for these terms is thin in every market. This is structural, not a settings bug.
  • It is not budgets or bid strategy. Dormant campaigns carry healthy $300 to $1,500/day budgets, and 53 of the 57 healthy campaigns run the exact same Target Spend strategy as the dormant ones. The difference is demand and auction traction, not configuration.
  • One campaign is simply switched off inside. Dallas Emergency is ENABLED at campaign level but its only ad group is PAUSED. It can never serve. It has $4,482 lifetime spend and 94 conversions. Easiest win on this list.

Methodology

Daily campaign metrics pulled via the Google Ads API (GAQL) for Jun 13 through Sep 11, 2026, joined against lifetime (all-time) campaign totals. "Dormant" means ENABLED campaign status with $0 cost in the last 7 days. "Lifetime" is the all-time figure returned by the API. Verified lead signal comes from CallRail calls tagged A-FLKickAss on PPC-number trackers over the same 90 days (452 calls). Campaign creation dates are not exposed by this API version, so campaign age is inferred from first observed activity and lifetime totals. Ad group, ad, and keyword statuses were spot-checked on the 12 highest-value dormant campaigns and fully checked for enabled ad groups across all 44.
