Answering your Dallas question directly

  • Call Only: Dallas Area yesterday: $571 on 3 clicks ($190 per click). Calls: it draws only on the shared call-only pool, which took 14 calls and 4 KA across all five call-only campaigns. Zero calls landed on a Dallas-only number, and none of the pool calls carried a click ID tying them to Dallas Area. So: no leads we can honestly pin on Dallas Area yesterday, KA or otherwise.
  • Call Only 674 ($2,743): 2 calls tied to it by click ID (1 KA) plus 1 KA form from a click on its ad (the dyed diesel landing page form). Plus an unknowable share of the pool's 14 calls. Even generously: worst money of the day.
  • Search Lead Gen (673) ($925): 1 call via click ID, and it was KA. Best cost-per-verified-lead among the big spenders at $925, which tells you how rough the day was.
  • The four re-enabled Diesels ($1,679 combined): zero calls on any number, zero forms. Pure spend.
  • GMB Midlothian spent little ($<66) and its number took 14 calls, 1 KA. The Maps campaigns stay cheap and busy; at least this one answers the phone.

The fix list stands, with sharper teeth

  • 1. 674: pause or hard cap. A third of the day's spend, 2 attributable leads, and it cannibalizes your geo call-only tests.
  • 2. Dallas Area needs a CPC cap or target. $190 clicks are a strategy problem, not a market problem.
  • 3. The four Diesels: $1,679, zero calls. Pause again or price the experiment knowingly.
  • 4. Number breakout matters more after today. The call-only pool makes per-campaign call attribution impossible. When you're ready to break numbers out per campaign (even just per call-only campaign), this report gets exact overnight.
