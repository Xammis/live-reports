Fuel Logic | Google Ads Change Review

July to August Event Trail

A team-ready account overview, followed by a detailed reconstruction of the Nationwide Call campaign experiment, rollback, and overcorrection.

Financials are USD. Dates use the Google Ads account timezone, America/Los_Angeles. Performance data runs through the last complete day, August 16, 2026. Current keyword status was checked August 17.

Core finding: the August rollback did not return the campaign to July normal.

The Nationwide Call campaign moved from 32 enabled broad keywords before the experiment, to 168 after expansion, then down to 13. The rollback left broad coverage 59% below the original baseline, and post-rollback daily spend fell 86% below July's average.

Operational effect

Post-rollback spend averaged $172.29 per day versus $1,239.86 in July, an 86% decline. Clicks averaged 4.3 per day versus 21.6, an 80% decline. The lower $129.22 CPA is based on only four conversions and is not yet stable.

5 | Team repair plan

Return to the baseline first, then test selectively

Restore 24 paused legacy broad terms

These were enabled before August 5 and were paused during the Aug. 14 rollback.

Recreate 4 removed legacy broad terms

These belonged to the original baseline but were hard-removed on Aug. 12.

Pause the 9 Aug. 5 additions still enabled

This produces the cleanest structural return to the original 32 broad-keyword set.

Hold a clean observation window

Avoid another bulk rewrite. Review spend, calls, conversion quality, CPA, and search terms daily for at least seven complete days before a selective retest.

Retest winners as a controlled cohort

If the team wants expansion, use a small labeled group with explicit budget, CPA, call-quality, and stop-loss thresholds.

6 | Interpretation limits

What this report does and does not prove

  • Google Ads conversions can post after the click, so the newest three-day CPA can change.
  • Account-wide July CPA combines different campaigns and conversion actions. It is context, not a direct benchmark for the call campaign.
  • This report reconstructs criterion status from current keyword inventory and Google Ads change events. It does not assign intent or blame to individual users.
  • Call quality, lead quality, revenue, and offline close rate were not available in this read-only audit.
  • No Google Ads changes were made.
