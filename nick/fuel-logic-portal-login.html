Client Portal Reskin

The new login page

The client portal sign-in has been rebuilt as a split screen: a washed-back fleet fueling photo with the Fuel Logic badge on one side, and a clean, brand-sized sign-in form on the other. On phones it collapses to just the form. Light and dark mode are both covered below, followed by the six main portal pages in light mode, which is now the default across the portal.

What changed

Every size, color and radius follows the Fuel Logic brand guide or the portal’s own brand-aligned controls.

Layout

  • Split screen on desktop: fleet fueling photo on the left half, sign-in form on the right.
  • On phones the photo panel is hidden and the form fills the screen.
  • Form sits on white in light mode so it stands apart from the photo side.

Photo

  • Fleet fueling scene at golden hour, with the worker in a white shirt.
  • Worker scaled up partway so he reads at a glance without dominating the frame.
  • Parked trucks, trees and building blurred further so they sit back as a soft backdrop.
  • Photo zoomed to 125% inside its panel.

Overlay and badge

  • Photo washed back at 85%: brand Light Grey (#E9E9E9) in light mode, #231F20 in dark mode.
  • Official Fuel Logic badge centered on the photo, raised to line up with the sign-in heading.
  • All-white badge in light mode, standard color badge in dark mode. No shadow.

Form

  • “Sign into your account” heading at the brand H3 size, centered under the logo and Client Portal label.
  • Username and password joined into one stacked group, with icons and placeholders in place of labels.
  • Fields and buttons rounded to the portal control radius (0.375rem).
  • Sign in button uses the brand button type: Work Sans 500 at 1.3rem.
  • “Forgot password?” moved below the Sign in button and centered.

Footer

  • Version and copyright centered and pinned to the bottom of the screen.
  • Both lines at the brand Small size (0.9rem), plain text with no pill.

One judgment call

The brand guide defines button type but not a corner radius for fields and buttons. These use 0.375rem, the same radius as the portal’s other inputs and primary buttons. The pre-rebuild login used a slightly tighter 0.25rem if that is preferred.
